# Quarantined — not real product photography

Every file in this folder is AI generated. It does not depict the watches you
are selling. Nothing in here is referenced by the site.

## How this was established

Look at any dial in `img/lineup-1200.webp`, `img/og.jpg`, or any frame in
`seq/`. The day ring reads `THON  TUE  TRU  SAT  THU  SAO  SRU`. The black
dial repeats `SAT` three times. The green dial reads `WIL  WED  WED  THE`
and `SRJ`.

No factory prints that. A day wheel is a stamped mass produced part; even the
cheapest quartz module carries MON TUE WED THU FRI SAT SUN, correctly spelled,
in order. Garbled text is the signature of image generation.

Corroborating tells in the same files: bracelet links dissolve and merge
mid-run, the gold piece has no crown and no subdial hands, subdial counts and
positions change between watches that are supposed to share one case design,
and the chapter ring numerals (`20 25 70 75 45 88 80 100 110 120 130 131`)
are not a scale that exists.

## Why it is quarantined rather than deleted

That is the owner's call, not the build's. Deleting is one command once the
decision is made.

## Why it can never ship

The brand's entire argument is that it does not borrow legitimacy. A buyer
pays for a piece, receives it, holds it next to the hero image and sees a
different object. On a twenty piece edition each buyer is five percent of the
customer base.

There is also exposure under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 for misleading
representation of goods, and Meta's advertising policies prohibit ads that
misrepresent the product. A rejected ad account against a budget with no
backup is a total loss.

## Replacing it

Real photographs go in `incoming/photos/`. See `incoming/README.md` for the
shot list. Until they exist the site renders its own materials and carries no
product photograph at all.
