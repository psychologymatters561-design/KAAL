01 · What KAAL is

A brand built around an ending

KAAL is an Indian watch brand. It designs and sells watches in closed, numbered series, and permanently retires each design once the final piece in that series has shipped.

Most brands treat a limited edition as a marketing position they can reverse. KAAL treats it as the product. When a series closes there is no second run, no Mark II, no anniversary reissue, and no quiet return in a different strap. The design is finished with.

Everything is sold direct from thekaal.co. There is no marketplace, no reseller and no third-party fulfilment in between.

02 · The name

काल means time — and the end of it

In Hindi and Sanskrit, काल — kaal — means time. The same word means death, an epoch, the end of a period. One word holds the duration and the ending both.

A brand named after time and its ending, selling designs it retires on purpose, is not a coincidence to be explained away in a footer. The full reasoning is set out in the manifesto.

03 · Who makes it

One person is answerable for it

KAAL is owned and operated by Arjun Dixit, who is also the person who answers the email. There is no support queue and no outsourced desk between a buyer and the brand.

Cases are finished by hand, each one worked individually by people who have spent their working lives doing this and nothing else — which is why no two are perfectly identical, and why that is the point. Every piece is opened, checked and closed again before it is boxed.

That process is the reason a series is small. Hand finishing and individual inspection do not scale by wanting them to.

The one part KAAL does not make is the movement, and it is named rather than glossed over: the Seiko Epson VX7N, a Japanese five hand multi function quartz calibre, in all twenty pieces, backed for twenty four months from dispatch. It is the only component in a watch that a photograph cannot show you, which is exactly why it has a page of its own.

04 · Which KAAL this is

Three other businesses trade under a version of this name

काल is an ordinary word in Hindi and Sanskrit, so more than one business has reached for it. Stated plainly, so that nothing here is read as theirs and nothing of theirs is read as ours:

  • Kaal Watch, Singapore — a Singaporean microbrand known for its Multiverse series. A separate, unrelated business.
  • Kala Watch Co, India — a different Indian watch brand, at kalawatches.com.
  • thekaal.com — sells clothing. A different company on a different domain.

This KAAL is thekaal.co: Indian, founded and run by Arjun Dixit, twenty numbered watches on a Seiko Epson VX7N. We make no claim about the others and have no comment on their work. What else you can verify before buying.

06 · How buying works

The rules, stated plainly

  • One piece exists per number. A number cannot be reserved or held — it is yours only once payment is captured.
  • Where two orders reach us for the same number, the earlier captured payment stands and the later one is refunded in full.
  • The price shown at checkout is the price payable. Shipping within India is included.
  • Payment is collected by Razorpay. KAAL never sees or stores card, UPI or banking credentials.
  • When the twentieth piece ships, Series 01 closes permanently.
