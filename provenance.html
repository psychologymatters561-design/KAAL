02 · Where the watch comes from

Named parts, a named inspection, and nothing in between

A Seiko Epson VX7N, backed twenty four months from dispatch, in a case finished by hand and opened, checked and closed again before it is boxed.

Every one of the twenty is inspected by KAAL before it ships. No order is passed to a third party warehouse or a supplier to fulfil, and nothing is dispatched that KAAL has not physically had in hand.

The movement is the test of this, which is why it has a page of its own. A brand that buys finished watches sight unseen and forwards the parcels cannot tell you whose movement is inside, because it does not know. It says "quartz movement" and moves on. Naming the supplier is a claim that can be checked against the watch in your hand within seven days, at no cost to you, which is precisely why it is worth making.

Sold direct, and only direct. There is no marketplace listing, no reseller, no authorised dealer and no third party seller. A KAAL watch offered anywhere other than thekaal.co did not come from us.

03 · Who takes the money

Razorpay takes it. We never see it

Payment is collected by Razorpay, an RBI authorised payment aggregator. Card, UPI and net banking credentials go directly to Razorpay and are never seen, handled or stored by KAAL. We receive your name, email, postal address, telephone number and the number you chose. That is the whole list.

What that means in practice: your payment sits inside a regulated system with its own dispute process, independent of anything KAAL does or fails to do. The price shown at checkout is the price payable — delivery within India is included and there is nothing added afterwards. The full privacy and payment terms are on the legal page.

04 · Why there are no reviews

Series 01 has not shipped. So there are none

There are no customer reviews, star ratings or testimonials anywhere on this site, and there will not be until people who have actually bought a piece have written them.

This is the easiest thing on the internet to manufacture and the fastest way to make a new brand look established. Five stars, four names, four stock portraits, thirty minutes' work. We have not done it and we will not, and the reason to say so here is that a site with no reviews looks thin until you understand it is a choice.

So: any review, rating or testimonial attributed to KAAL today did not come from a KAAL customer. If you find one, it is not ours, and we would like to know where it is.

When the first pieces have shipped and buyers write to us, what they write will go up — attributed, with the number they own, and unedited.

06 · What is not published yet

The gaps, stated by us rather than found by you

Two things a careful buyer would look for on this site are not on it. Here they are, named, with the reason.

The address itself goes to any buyer or prospective buyer who asks at connect@thekaal.co — before you pay, not after. We would rather tell you the omission is deliberate than let you find it and wonder.

There are no social accounts yet

No Instagram, no YouTube, no listing anywhere. There is one website, one email address and one telephone number, and everything the brand has said is on the four pages of this site. Accounts will exist; they do not yet, and a row of empty profiles would have been easy to create and would have proved nothing.

Neither gap is an argument for buying. They are here because the alternative — saying nothing and hoping — is what we would be doing if we had something to hide. Twenty watches is a small enough number to sell to twenty people who checked.
