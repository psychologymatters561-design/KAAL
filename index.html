Before you buy

The six things people actually ask us.

+Is this just a generic watch with a new name on the dial?

The case, the bezel, the dial and the bracelet are ours, drawn for this edition. The movement is a hand wound mechanical calibre we buy in and regulate before it ships, which is what almost every independent watch brand under a lakh does. We would rather write that down than let you find out later.

+Will the rose gold wear off?

It is ion plating bonded into the steel, not a sprayed coat. Daily wear, shirt cuffs and a desk will not lift it. If it does flake inside two years we replace the case and you pay nothing.

+What happens if it stops working?

Two years on the movement, replaced or repaired free. After that we service it for life at whatever the parts cost us. You write to connect@thekaal.co and a person answers, usually the same day. There is no ticket number and no call centre.

+Is 38 mm too small for a man?

No. 38 mm is the size a dress watch is supposed to be, and it is the size most vintage watches worth owning were. It fits a 6 to 7.5 inch wrist and slides under a shirt cuff without catching. If your wrist is over 7.5 inches, write to us first and we will tell you honestly.

+Do I really have to wind it every day?

Yes. Around thirty seconds, once a day, usually while you are getting dressed. If you forget for a weekend it stops and you simply wind it again. Nothing breaks. Some people find this annoying. Most of the people who buy this find it is the reason they bought it.

+How fast does it ship, and what if I hate it?

Dispatched within 48 hours, anywhere in India, free. Seven days from the day it lands to send it back for a full refund, worn or unworn, no questions typed into a form. We only ask that the box comes back with it.
