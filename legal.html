Privacy

We collect the name, email address, postal address and telephone number you give at checkout, the number you choose from the edition, and a gift note if you write one. That is all we hold about you as a buyer.

We use it to take your order, dispatch your watch, and answer you if you write to us. We do not sell it, rent it, or pass it to anyone beyond the courier carrying your parcel and the payment processor taking your money.

  • Payments. Card, UPI and banking details go directly to Razorpay and are never seen or stored by us. Razorpay handles them under its own privacy policy.
  • Your browser. The site stores the number you pick in your own browser so the confirmation page can greet you by it. It never leaves your device and is cleared once shown.
  • Gift notes. A gift note is used for one thing: writing the card that goes in the box. The price is never printed in a gift order.
  • Measurement and advertising. This site uses the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics to count visits and to measure whether our advertising works — for example, that a page was viewed or an order was placed. These services set cookies and receive technical data such as your IP address and browser, under their own privacy policies. When an order is paid, we also tell Meta that a purchase happened, with your email address and telephone number in a one-way scrambled (hashed) form so it can be matched to an ad without being readable. We never send them your card details, your address or your gift note. You can block them with your browser's tracking protection or an ad blocker, and the site works exactly the same.
  • Retention. Order records are kept as long as needed to honour the warranty and meet tax and accounting obligations.

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to connect@thekaal.co.
